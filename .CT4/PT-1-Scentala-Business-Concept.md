## 1. Brand Name

**SCENTALA** — A direct fusion of "Scent" and the Tagalog word "Tala," meaning star.

## 2. Brand Story

### The Inspiration
We noticed a common problem: finding a perfume that lasts all day usually costs too much money. Most Filipinos are left choosing between expensive luxury brands or cheap body sprays that disappear in the heat after just an hour. Scentala fixes this by giving you a high-quality, long-lasting scent you can wear every day without spending a lot.

### Our Mission
To formulate climate-resilient, oil-rich fragrances tailored for the Filipino lifestyle, empowering students and young professionals to project an unforgettable olfactory presence every single day.

### Our Vision
To become the top local perfume brand in the Philippines, loved for giving luxury-quality scents at prices everyone can afford.

### Our Values
Honest pricing, all-day lasting power, made for hot weather, and dependable quality.

## 3. Tagline

> “A scent that outshines the rest.”

## 4. Main Products

### Signature Eau de Parfum (EDP) – 50ml
Our main perfume line. It is made with high oil content so the scent stays strong on your skin even when you sweat in hot and humid weather.

### The "Tala" Pocket-Puff Atomizers – 10ml
Small, sturdy, refillable spray bottles you can easily carry in your pocket or bag for a quick spray during daily travel.

## 5. Target Market / Audience

- **Core Demographic:** People aged 18–35 years old (students and young adults).
- **Location:** Major cities in the Philippines, sold mainly through online shops and social media.
- **Interests & Lifestyle:** College students and young office workers who care about looking good, smelling clean, and staying fresh every day.
- **The Specific Need:** A great-smelling perfume that can survive heavy traffic, crowded commutes, and hot weather without needing to spray again and again.

## 6. Competitor Analysis

### Direct Competitors

- **Cheap Perfume Dupe Brands:** They offer many cheap copies of popular scents.

  - **Scentala’s Advantage:** We make our own original scents with higher oil concentration, made specifically not to fade in the heat.

- **Expensive Niche Boutiques:** They sell unique, high-status perfumes at very high prices.

  - **Scentala’s Advantage:** We match their quality and lasting power, but at a price you can actually afford for everyday use.

### Indirect Competitors

- **Imported Designer Perfumes:** Famous brand names that are too pricey for regular daily wear.

  - **Scentala’s Advantage:** Much lower price, easy local stock, and a formula tested for Philippine weather.

## 7. Value Proposition (Why SCENTALA?)

Scentala gives you premium-quality perfume at a price anyone can afford. You no longer have to waste money on weak body sprays or spend too much on luxury brands. With scents made to beat the Philippine heat, Scentala makes smelling fresh and feeling confident an easy daily habit.
