# Creative Task #4: Brand Presentation

## What Is a Brand Presentation?

A brand presentation is a detailed overview of a business’s identity, values, and vision, designed to communicate how the brand connects with its target audience.

## Task Overview

For this Creative Task, create a brand presentation that showcases all the work completed in:

- **PT #1:** Business Concept Development
- **CT #1:** Logo Design
- **CT #2:** First Ever Brand Post
- **CT #3:** Promotional Event Poster

The presentation should effectively communicate your brand’s identity, values, and vision using visuals, consistent typography, and color schemes.

The goal is to connect your brand with its target audience in a cohesive and engaging way.

## Presentation Requirements

### 1. Performance Task #1 — Business Concept Development

Give a brief presentation about the company. Include:

- Company or brand name and tagline
- Mission
- Target audience or market
- Sample products or services

### 2. Creative Task #1 — Logo Design

Present your primary and secondary logo designs in the format shown in the provided example.

Include:

- The logo sketch
- Color schemes or palette
- Typography

### 3. Creative Task #2 — First Ever Brand Post

Show your first official brand post as part of your social media presence or campaign.

### 4. Creative Task #3 — Promotional Event Poster

Describe your thought process for this task. Explain how you came up with the event and its poster design.

Include:

- Color schemes or palette
- Typography

## Notes

- There is no specific format for the presentation, so feel free to be creative in showcasing what your brand truly represents.
- Focus on engaging visuals and clarity to effectively communicate your brand’s essence.

## Brand Presentation Rubric

| Criteria | Description | Percentage |
|---|---|---:|
| **Content** | Clarity and relevance of the information presented, including brand identity, values, and vision. | 30% |
| **Visual Design** | Use of visuals, typography, color schemes, and overall aesthetic that aligns with the brand identity. | 25% |
| **Creativity** | Originality in presentation style, including innovative methods to showcase the brand. | 20% |
| **Engagement** | Ability to connect with the audience, including interaction and keeping their attention throughout. | 15% |
| **Delivery** | Presentation skills, including clarity of speech, confidence, and pacing. | 10% |

---

**Total: 100%**
